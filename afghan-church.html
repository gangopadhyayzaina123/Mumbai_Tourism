Afghan Church

Description

The Church of St John the Evangelist, popularly known as Afghan Church, is a historic Anglican church in Mumbai. It is famous for its Gothic Revival architecture, tall spire and beautiful stained-glass windows.

History

The church was built in memory of the soldiers who died during the First Anglo-Afghan War and the subsequent military campaign in Sindh during the 19th century.

Construction began in 1847 and the church was consecrated in 1858. The building was designed in the Gothic Revival style and became an important landmark in the Colaba area.

Location

Navy Nagar, Colaba, Mumbai, Maharashtra.
