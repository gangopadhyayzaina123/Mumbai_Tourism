Elephanta Caves

Description

The Elephanta Caves are a group of ancient rock-cut caves located on Elephanta Island in Mumbai Harbour. The caves are famous for their impressive stone sculptures and temples dedicated mainly to Lord Shiva.

History

The caves were created by cutting and carving the solid rock of the island. They contain sculptures and architectural features dating mainly from the period between the 5th and 6th centuries.

The most famous sculpture in the caves is the Trimurti, a large three-faced representation of Shiva. The Elephanta Caves are recognized for their historical, artistic and religious importance and were declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1987.

Location

Elephanta Island, Mumbai Harbour, Maharashtra.
