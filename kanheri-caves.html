Kanheri Caves

Description

The Kanheri Caves are a group of ancient rock-cut caves located inside Sanjay Gandhi National Park in Mumbai. The caves contain Buddhist sculptures, monasteries, prayer halls and inscriptions carved into the basalt rock.

History

The Kanheri Caves developed over several centuries, beginning around the 1st century BCE. They became an important Buddhist centre and continued to be used and expanded for many centuries.

The caves contain numerous examples of Buddhist architecture and art. Their location near ancient trade routes helped Kanheri become an important centre for Buddhist learning, meditation and religious activities.

Location

Sanjay Gandhi National Park, Borivali, Mumbai, Maharashtra.
