Mani Bhavan

Description

Mani Bhavan is a historic building in Mumbai associated with Mahatma Gandhi. It is now a museum and memorial that preserves important information and objects related to Gandhi's life and his activities in Mumbai.

History

Mani Bhavan served as Mahatma Gandhi's Mumbai headquarters from 1917 to 1934. During this period, Gandhi was involved in several important activities connected with India's freedom movement.

The building contains photographs, books, personal belongings and exhibits related to Gandhi. It also preserves the room where Gandhi stayed and worked, making it an important historical site in Mumbai.

Location

Laburnum Road, Gamdevi, Mumbai, Maharashtra.
