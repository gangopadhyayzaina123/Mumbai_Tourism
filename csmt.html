Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus

Explore the history and character of this remarkable Mumbai landmark.

Description

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus, commonly known as CSMT, is one of Mumbai's most famous historic buildings. It is also an important railway station and is known for its grand architecture.

History

The building was constructed during the British period and was formerly known as Victoria Terminus. It was designed by British architect Frederick William Stevens and was completed in 1888.

The architecture combines Victorian Gothic Revival elements with traditional Indian architectural features. Because of its historical and architectural importance, CSMT was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2004.
