Rajabai Clock Tower

Explore the history and character of this remarkable Mumbai landmark.

Description

The Rajabai Clock Tower is one of Mumbai's most recognizable historic landmarks. It stands within the University of Mumbai campus and is known for its tall structure and impressive Gothic architectural design.

History

The tower was designed by British architect Sir George Gilbert Scott and was completed in 1878. It was funded partly by Premchand Roychand, a prominent businessman and founder of the Bombay Stock Exchange.

The tower was named after Premchand Roychand's mother, Rajabai. Its architecture combines Gothic and Venetian influences and includes detailed stonework and decorative elements.
