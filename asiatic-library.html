Asiatic Library

Description

The Asiatic Library is one of Mumbai's historic buildings and is home to the Asiatic Society of Mumbai. It is located near the Horniman Circle area and is known for its grand neoclassical architecture.

History

The Asiatic Society of Mumbai was established in 1804 as an institution dedicated to the study and preservation of knowledge, literature and historical materials.

The library building is known for its large staircase, classical columns and impressive architectural design. Its collection includes rare books, manuscripts, maps and other historical materials, making it an important part of Mumbai's cultural heritage.

Location

Town Hall, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Fort, Mumbai, Maharashtra.
